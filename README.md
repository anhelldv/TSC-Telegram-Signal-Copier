## 📖 About

**TSC Signal Copier** is a Windows desktop application that connects your 
Telegram channels to MetaTrader 5 and executes trading signals 
automatically. Unlike typical signal copiers that only forward orders, 
TSC analyzes every provider's performance in real time, warns you when a 
channel is losing steam, and blocks dangerous trades before they affect 
your account.

You don't just copy signals. You trade with data.

## ✨ Features

### 🧠 Intelligent Analysis
- **Automatic provider scoring** (0-100) based on win rate, profit factor, 
  consistency, and volume
- **Real-time insights** — human-readable alerts about your providers
- **Top Performer detection** with trend charts
- **Complete trade history** with CSV export

### 🛡️ Capital Protection
- **SL/TP policy** — block, auto-add, or allow signals without stop loss
- **Daily/Weekly/Monthly limits** with automatic position closure
- **Maximum drawdown protection**
- **Maximum concurrent trades** and rate limits per period
- **News filter** for high-impact events
- **Trading hours filter** with weekend auto-close

### 🎨 Premium Interface
- **Splash screen** with smooth animations
- **Aurora Glass theme** (violet + cyan gradients)
- **Modern dashboard** with animated KPIs
- **Live chart** with equity and balance history
- **Dark mode UI** by default

### ⚡ Native Performance
- **No WebView2 required**
- **Local SQLite database** — your data stays on your PC
- **Fast startup** and async query engine
- **Persistent avatars** and configuration

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need MetaTrader 5 installed?**  
A: Yes. TSC Signal Copier works in pair with MT5 via a local signal file.

**Q: Can I use it on Mac or Linux?**  
A: No. Windows only. On Mac/Linux, use a Windows VPS.

**Q: Do I need to keep the app running 24/7?**  
A: Yes. Use a VPS for continuous operation.

**Q: Does it work with any broker?**  
A: Yes, any broker that supports MT5.

**Q: How does the free trial work?**  
A: 3 days of full access with your MetaQuotes ID. One trial per device.

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes. All data stays local on your PC.
